My Fitness Routine Cards

A simple weekly routine, one card per habit

This page shows a simple weekly fitness routine. Each card covers one habit, how to do it, and how long it takes.

Morning Stretch

Loosen up with gentle stretches for your arms, legs, back, and neck to wake the body up.

8 minutes

Cardio Blast

Jumping jacks, jogging on the spot, or skipping rope to raise your heart rate and build stamina.

22 minutes

Strength Training

Squats, push-ups, and lunges to build functional strength across your whole body.

18 minutes

Core Blast

Planks, bicycle crunches, and leg raises to strengthen your core and improve posture.

12 minutes

Yoga Flow

A short flow of poses to build balance, flexibility, and a calmer mind.

15 minutes

Evening Walk

A relaxed outdoor walk to unwind, get fresh air, and stay active without overexertion.

30 minutes

Fitness Tip: A good routine includes movement, rest, water, and consistency — remember to hydrate well throughout the day, especially after a workout.
