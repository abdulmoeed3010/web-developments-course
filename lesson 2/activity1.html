Fact 2: Lightning Strikes

Lightning strikes the Earth about 100 times every second, which is around 8 million times a day.

how hot is lightning?

Lightning can reach temperatures of around 30,000 kelvins (53,540 degrees Fahrenheit), which is hotter than the surface of the sun.

can lightning strike twice?

Yes, lightning can strike the same place multiple times, especially if it's a tall, isolated object like a skyscraper or a tree.

Fact 3: The Deep Ocean

The deep ocean is one of the least explored places on Earth, with vast areas still unknown to humans.

How deep the ocean go?

The deepest part of the ocean is the Mariana Trench, which reaches a depth of about 36,070 feet (10,994 meters).
