Ride Dictionary

Canyon Zip
A 400-metre zipline that crosses the widest part of the gorge.
Rockface Climb
A guided vertical climb up the park's tallest sandstone wall.
Rapids Run
A raft ride through fast water and narrow rock channels.

Safety Briefing

All riders check in at Basecamp Gate before their first activity.

The water rescue drill takes place at Rapids Dock.

Ride Commands

Launch the zipline: zip.launch("Canyon Zip")

Begin the climb: climb.start("Rockface Climb")

Halt the raft: raft.stop("Rapids Run")

Park Short Forms

CRSC inspects every line and harness each morning.

WRT stands by at every water ride.

JRP is for visitors under 12.
