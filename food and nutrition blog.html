Building a Healthy Plate

Understanding a Balanced Meal

A balanced meal consists of a variety of food groups, including fruits, vegetables, grains, protein, and dairy. Each group provides essential nutrients that support overall health.

Learn more about healthy eating here: ChooseMyPlate.gov

Healthy Snacking

Healthy snacks can include fruits, vegetables, nuts, and yogurt. Avoid processed snacks that are high in sugar and unhealthy fats.

For more snack ideas, visit: Healthline: Healthy Snacks

Nutrition Tips for Students

Stay Hydrated

Drinking enough water is essential for maintaining energy and focus throughout the day. Aim for at least 8 glasses of water daily.

Eat a Variety of Foods

Incorporate a wide range of foods in your diet to ensure you get all the necessary nutrients. Include different colors of fruits and vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins.

Read more about healthy food choices here: Academy of Nutrition and Dietetics
