🐚 Sunny Shallows

Wade through warm, sunlit water near the shore and spot starfish, hermit crabs, sea turtles, and tiny clownfish.

Did you know? Starfish don't have blood — they pump nutrients using seawater instead!

🐠 Coral Reef Adventure

Dive into a colourful coral reef and discover parrotfish, seahorses, octopuses, and gliding manta rays.

Did you know? Coral reefs are built by tiny living animals called coral polyps!

🐙 Deep Sea Mystery

Descend into the dark deep ocean and search for anglerfish, giant squid, jellyfish, and glowing plankton.

Did you know? Some deep-sea creatures make their own light through a process called bioluminescence!
