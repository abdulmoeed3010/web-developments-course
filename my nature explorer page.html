My Nature Explorer Page

Wander through forests, rivers, deserts, oceans, and mountains, and meet the wildlife that calls each place home.

Forest Adventure

Beneath a canopy of towering trees, forests buzz with birdsong and rustling leaves. They filter our air, shelter countless creatures, and keep the whole planet breathing easy.

River Journey

Rivers wind their way across the land, carrying fresh water to towns, farms, and forests alike. Along their banks, fish, birds, and plants thrive in a constantly moving world.

Desert Discovery

Deserts may look empty, but they're full of life built to survive the heat — cacti storing water, foxes hunting at night, and sand dunes that shift with every gust of wind.

Ocean Depths

Covering most of our planet, oceans hide coral reefs, drifting jellyfish, and creatures we've barely begun to discover. Every wave carries a story from somewhere far away.

Mountain View

Rising high above the clouds, mountains offer sweeping views and thin, crisp air. Hardy plants and sure-footed animals make their home on these rocky slopes.

Wildlife Zone

From tiny insects to great herds of grazing animals, wildlife thrives when its habitat is left undisturbed. Protecting their homes means protecting the balance of nature itself.

Nature Explorer Checklist

  • Observe plants and animals quietly, from a distance
  • Stay on marked trails and paths
  • Pack out any trash you bring in
  • Carry water and dress for the weather

Nature Tip: Protecting nature means protecting our future.
